This project focuses on **Summary Fidelity and Report Scaffolding** using Generative AI in the aviation industry. The main objective is to learn how AI can summarise workplace information while ensuring that the summary remains accurate, complete and faithful to the original source.

The activity begins with an airport passenger-service report containing **120 passenger comments**. Students use a structured prompt to create a short summary while retaining important figures related to security queues, directional signs, baggage delays, seating and positive staff feedback. The AI-generated summary is then checked claim by claim to identify whether each statement is fully supported, partly supported or unsupported by the source.

Students also perform a separate **numerical verification** to ensure that important figures have not been changed or omitted. They identify possible omissions, distortions and inventions, such as unsupported satisfaction percentages, financial impacts, airport comparisons or claims about the success of a temporary information-desk trial.

The next part of the lab focuses on correcting the AI summary through a targeted follow-up prompt. Students then create a **management-report scaffold** with sections such as Executive Summary, Key Findings, Actions Taken, Limitations, Recommendations and Conclusion. The use of **`[VERIFY]` placeholders** helps identify information that requires additional evidence before the report can be finalised.

A second activity uses an airline baggage-complaint report containing **80 complaints**, allowing students to practise checking figures and preventing incorrect claims such as describing delayed baggage as permanently lost.

Finally, students create their own summarisation prompt, complete peer review and answer reflection questions. The complete work is documented in `summary-fidelity-and-report-scaffold.md` and uploaded to the `genai-business-portfolio` GitHub repository. The project highlights the importance of **source accuracy, numerical verification, limitations, human review and responsible use of AI** in aviation management reporting.
