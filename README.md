DAY 9 — LAB 9
Marketing Copy and Compliance
Programme: BBA Aviation Management
Course: Generative AI for Business
Tools: ChatGPT, Google Gemini and GitHub
Technical Requirement: No coding required
Objective
To transform one verified aviation-business brief into platform-specific marketing content and evaluate AI-generated copy for accuracy, brand consistency, audience suitability and compliance.
Learning Outcomes
After completing this lab, students will be able to:
Convert a business brief into structured marketing prompts.
Create content for different communication platforms.
Adapt tone, length and format for each audience.
Distinguish factual claims from promotional language.
Identify misleading, exaggerated and unsupported claims.
Rewrite non-compliant marketing content.
Apply a marketing-content review checklist.
Maintain consistency across multiple channels.
Upload a reviewed marketing portfolio to GitHub.

Step 2 — Read the Aviation Campaign Brief
Campaign Brief
A fictional airline, AeroVista, is launching a new student-fare campaign.
Verified information:
Campaign name: AeroVista Student Saver
Target audience: College and university students
Available routes: Selected domestic routes
Discount: Up to 10% on the base fare
Booking period: 1–15 October
Travel period: 15 October–15 December
Eligibility: Valid student identification required
Baggage, taxes, cancellation and change conditions: Subject to applicable fare rules
Seats: Limited and subject to availability
Booking channel: Official AeroVista website and mobile application
The brief does not state:
That every student will receive a 10% discount
That the offer applies to all routes
That baggage is free
That tickets are refundable
That there are no additional charges
That the offer is the cheapest available fare
That seats are guaranteed

Suggested Master Prompt
Act as a marketing communications executive for the fictional airline AeroVista. Using only the verified campaign brief provided, create platform-specific promotional content for the AeroVista Student Saver campaign. The target audience is college and university students. Do not claim that the offer applies to every route, that all students will receive the maximum discount, that baggage is free, that tickets are refundable or that seats are guaranteed. Clearly mention that the offer applies to selected domestic routes, requires valid student identification and is subject to availability and applicable fare rules. Do not invent additional benefits, prices or policy conditions.
